Use your project’s SCC or research-computing support channel for access,
workload, storage, or registry problems. Start with the matching
[troubleshooting entry](/troubleshooting/) and include:

- project and workload name;
- approximate time and current workload state;
- image tag and selected compute-resource preset;
- a short, safe log excerpt and actions already tried.

Do not include passwords, private keys, access tokens, personal contact details,
or sensitive dataset contents.
