first prevents the most common failed submissions.

<Prerequisites title="New-user checklist">
  <li>You are eligible to use the TU/e research-computing service.</li>
  <li>You can sign in to the <a href="https://spike.run.ai/">Run:ai portal</a> with your TU/e account.</li>
  <li>The intended project appears in the project selector and your role allows workload creation.</li>
  <li>You are a member of the corresponding Harbor project and Podman is installed on your build machine.</li>
  <li>EduVPN is connected whenever your network location requires it.</li>
</Prerequisites>

## Official service context

For institutional context, service boundaries, and SCC announcements, see the
[TU/e Supercomputing Spike-1 page](https://supercomputing.tue.nl/spike-1/).
Use this wiki for the practical researcher workflow; the institutional page is
the source for service-level announcements.

## Essential vocabulary

- **Project**: the boundary for members, workloads, data sources, and allocated compute.
- **Environment**: a reusable workload configuration that identifies an image and runtime settings.
- **Compute resource**: a project-approved preset for GPU, CPU, and memory requests.
- **Data source**: storage made available to a project or workload.
- **Preemptible workload**: work that may be stopped so higher-priority work can run; it must tolerate retries.
