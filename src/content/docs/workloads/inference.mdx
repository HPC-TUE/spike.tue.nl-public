Inference on Spike is deployed and managed for you by SCC AI Engineers. If a
model must remain available behind a serving endpoint, discuss the requirement
with your SCC AI Engineer. They will determine the appropriate deployment,
resources, access controls, health checks, and cleanup policy with you.

:::caution
Do not create, change, restart, or delete inference workloads yourself. Ask an
SCC AI Engineer to make these changes for you.
:::

## What to prepare

Before meeting with the AI Engineer:

1. Confirm that the model and its license are suitable for the project.
2. Record the model revision, expected usage, and intended users or client
   applications.
3. Prepare an immutable Harbor image tag, or discuss what the serving image
   needs to contain.
4. Identify any runtime tokens or other secrets. These must be provided through
   the approved secret mechanism, never baked into an image or command line.
5. Describe the expected request volume, response-time needs, and how long the
   endpoint is required.

The AI Engineer will create and validate the inference workload, including its
compute-resource preset, replicas, endpoint, authentication, health checks, and
request limits. Contact them again when the deployment needs to change or can
be removed.

:::note
Hosted-model availability and quotas change. Check the current service status
and usage guidance instead of copying a temporary model name or quota into a
workload definition.
:::
