Distributed training is a workload architecture, not only a larger GPU
request. Validate the one-GPU command first, then align process count, visible
devices, rendezvous settings, checkpointing, and selected Run:ai resources.

## Recovery requirements

- Persist checkpoints outside ephemeral storage.
- Make initialization retry-safe and record the exact image tag.
- Capture rank, world size, node placement, and first failure in logs.
- Stop a test when the launcher repeatedly fails rather than consuming quota.

FSDP, Torch Elastic, and low-precision kernels remain Experimental references
until they have a current image, resource preset, distributed startup test, and
checkpoint-recovery result.
